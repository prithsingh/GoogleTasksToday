# Privacy Policy — Today View for Google Tasks

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Today View for Google Tasks ("the extension") does not collect, store, sell or share any user data.

## What the extension accesses

The extension runs only on `tasks.google.com` and `calendar.google.com`. To build the Today view, it reads the tasks already shown on the page (titles, due dates and list names) inside your browser, and shows or hides them.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It makes no network requests.
- It does not save any data on your device or in your browser.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- It does not access your Google account, Google's APIs or any other website.

All processing happens on your device and stops when you close or leave the page.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
